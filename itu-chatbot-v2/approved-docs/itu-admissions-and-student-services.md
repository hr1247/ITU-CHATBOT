# ITU Admissions, Scholarships, and Student Services

Source: https://itu.edu.pk/admissions/

Information Technology University (ITU) Lahore conducts admissions on a strictly merit-based framework across all degree programs.

## Admissions Guidelines and Procedure
1. **Application Submission:** Applications are submitted online through the ITU Admissions Portal (available at https://itu.edu.pk).
2. **Eligibility Criteria:**
   * **Undergraduate (BS Programs):** Minimum 50% to 60% marks in Intermediate (FSc Pre-Engineering, ICS, Pre-Medical with math additional, or equivalent A-Levels/IB).
   * **Graduate (MS Programs):** Minimum 16 years of education (BS/MSc or equivalent) in relevant disciplines with a minimum 2.5/4.0 CGPA or 60% in annual system.
   * **PhD Programs:** Minimum 18 years of education (MS/MPhil or equivalent) with minimum 3.0/4.0 CGPA.
3. **Entry Tests Accepted:**
   * ITU conducts its own University Admission Test (ITU Test).
   * Accepted standardized test alternatives (depending on program): NAT (NTS), USAT (HEC), HAT, GAT General/Subject, SAT, GRE, or GMAT.

## Scholarships and Financial Assistance
ITU is committed to ensuring that financial limitations do not deter talented students:
* **ITU Merit Scholarships:** Awarded to top-performing students in the admission merit list and semester-wise GPA toppers.
* **Need-Based Financial Aid:** Tuition fee waivers and stipends for financially deserving students.
* **External Scholarships:** HEC Need-Based Scholarships, Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarships, Punjab Educational Endowment Fund (PEEF), and corporate sponsorships.

## Student Services & Campus Life
* **Student Services & Placement Office (SSPO):** Provides career counseling, corporate internship placements, resume building, and campus recruitment drives.
* **Student Societies & Clubs:** Includes Robotics Society, ACM Student Chapter, IEEE Student Branch, Media & Literary Society, Sports Club, and Women in Tech.
* **Campus Facilities:** High-speed Wi-Fi, modern computing labs, state-of-the-art library, digital study spaces, cafeteria, executive conference halls, and access to tech incubator resources at Arfa Software Technology Park.
