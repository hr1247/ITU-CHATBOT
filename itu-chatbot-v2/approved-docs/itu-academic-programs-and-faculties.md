# ITU Academic Programs and Faculties

Source: https://itu.edu.pk/academics/

The Information Technology University (ITU) Lahore offers a wide range of undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral degree programs across multiple specialized faculties.

## 1. Faculty of Computer Science & Information Technology
ITU's computing programs are accredited in the highest "W" category by the National Computing Education and Accreditation Council (NCEAC).

### Undergraduate Programs
* **BS Computer Science (BSCS):** 4-year, 8-semester program covering algorithms, system design, artificial intelligence, software engineering, databases, and theoretical computer science.
* **BS Software Engineering (BSSE):** Focused on large-scale software architecture, software quality engineering, DevOps, design patterns, and enterprise computing systems.
* **BS Artificial Intelligence (BSAI):** Specialized degree focusing on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), robotics, and data analytics.

### Graduate & PhD Programs
* **MS Computer Science (MSCS):** 2-year advanced degree with research thesis or project options.
* **MS Data Science (MSDS):** Interdisciplinary program focusing on big data architectures, predictive modeling, statistical learning, and business analytics.
* **MS Cybersecurity:** Specialized program focusing on information security, cryptography, network forensics, vulnerability assessment, and cloud security.
* **PhD Computer Science:** Rigorous research doctorate covering specialized computing disciplines.

---

## 2. Faculty of Engineering
All engineering programs are recognized and accredited by the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC).

### Undergraduate Programs
* **BS Electrical Engineering (BSEE):** 4-year degree covering electronics, telecommunications, signal processing, power systems, and embedded computing.
* **BS Computer Engineering (BSCE):** Bridge between computer science and electrical engineering, focusing on microprocessor design, hardware-software co-design, VLSI, and IoT architectures.

### Graduate & PhD Programs
* **MS Electrical Engineering (MSEE):** Advanced study in microelectronics, power electronics, RF systems, and wireless communications.
* **MS Computer Engineering:** Advanced hardware and embedded systems research.
* **PhD Electrical Engineering:** Doctoral research under internationally acclaimed faculty.

---

## 3. Faculty of Business and Management Sciences
Designed to cultivate technology-driven business leaders, entrepreneurs, and quantitative analysts.

### Undergraduate Programs
* **BS Management and Technology (BSMT):** Integrates business administration, technology management, organizational leadership, and digital transformation.
* **BS Financial Technology (BS FinTech):** Blends finance, blockchain, financial modeling, machine learning in finance, and quantitative trading.
* **BS Economics with Data Science:** Quantitative economics combined with statistical computing and econometric analysis.

### Graduate Programs
* **MS Management and Technology (MSMT)**
* **Executive MBA (EMBA):** Designed for mid-career professionals seeking executive leadership in technology and industry.

---

## 4. Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences
Focuses on the intersection of public administration, governance, digital policy, and social impact.

### Graduate Programs
* **MS Public Policy and Society:** Focuses on technology-driven public governance, policy analysis, and regulatory frameworks.
* **MS Development Studies: Development, Technology and Policy:** Explores modern socio-economic development, ICT for development, and sustainability.
