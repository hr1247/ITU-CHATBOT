## Academic Leadership Structure (Deans vs. Administrative Staff)
- **Faculty Deans** oversee entire Faculties (Engineering, Sciences, Business & Management Sciences, and Humanities & Social Sciences), while **Department Chairpersons** lead individual academic departments.
- **Administrative Officers** (such as Deputy Registrars, Program Managers in ORIC, Manager IT, Network Engineers, HR Officers, and Office Assistants) belong to university administration and are **not** Faculty Deans or Department Chairpersons.
