## Key Faculty Profiles: Dr. Rehan Hafiz vs. Dr. Rehan Ahmed
- **Dr. Rehan Hafiz (Computer Science / Computer Vision & AI):** Professor in the Department of Computer Science at ITU Lahore (PhD in Electrical Engineering from Queen Mary University of London). His research focuses on Computer Vision, Image & Video Processing, Virtual/Augmented Reality (VR/AR), and Artificial Intelligence, and he leads the Computer Vision & Graphics Lab.
- **Dr. Rehan Ahmed (Electrical Engineering / Embedded Systems & Hardware):** Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at ITU Lahore (PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology, USA). His research focuses on Embedded Systems, VLSI, Computer Architecture, Hardware Security, and FPGA/ASIC design, and he leads the Embedded Computing & Hardware Systems research group.
- **Key Comparison (Dr. Rehan Hafiz vs. Dr. Rehan Ahmed):** Both are senior faculty members at ITU Lahore, but they belong to different departments and research domains—**Dr. Rehan Hafiz** is in **Computer Science** specializing in Computer Vision, AI, and Graphics (PhD from Queen Mary University of London), whereas **Dr. Rehan Ahmed** is in **Electrical Engineering** specializing in Embedded Systems, Computer Architecture, and VLSI/Hardware (PhD from Georgia Tech).

## About ITU
The Information Technology University (ITU) of the Punjab is a premier public research university located in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. Founded to advance higher education and cutting-edge research in science, technology, engineering, and management, ITU models its interdisciplinary curricula and research culture on top global institutions.

## Accreditation & Recognition
- Chartered by the Government of the Punjab.
- Fully recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan.
- Computer Science and Computing programs are accredited in the highest "W" category by the National Computing Education and Accreditation Council (NCEAC).
- Engineering programs are recognized and accredited by the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC).

## Vision and Mission
- **Vision:** To become a globally recognized center of excellence in science, technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
- **Mission:** To nurture forward-looking scientists, engineers, tech entrepreneurs, and leaders through world-class instruction, rigorous research, and deep industry partnerships.
