# Information Technology University (ITU) Lahore — Campus Locations & Addresses

ITU operates two main campuses in Lahore, Pakistan:

## 1. Main Campus (Arfa Software Technology Park)
- **Address:** 6th Floor, Arfa Software Technology Park (ASTP), 346-B, Ferozepur Road, Model Town, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan.
- **Key Offices & Departments:** Admissions Office, Registrar Office, Main Administrative Offices, and select academic facilities.
- **Landmark:** Located at Arfa Kareem Tower on main Ferozepur Road near Model Town / Kalma Chowk.

## 2. Barki Road Campus (Permanent Main Campus)
- **Address:** Information Technology University (ITU), Main Barki Road, near Paragon City / DHA Phase 8, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan (accessible via Lahore Ring Road Barki Interchange).
- **Key Facilities:** Academic blocks, classrooms, research labs, student facilities, and faculty offices.
- **Directions:** Accessible via Barki Road heading east from Paragon City and the Lahore Ring Road Barki Interchange.
